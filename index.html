white-blue-flyers.dck (deck file, .dck format)

A different deck from the same cards: ten flyers and a surveil theme. Saheeli turns every surveil into a Thopter, and Way of the Mind Sculptor and Campus Crier make Jace planeswalkers that surveil and draw. It has less removal than the black-green decks, so it wins by evasion and card quality. Tap any card to see it full size.

white-red.dck (deck file, .dck format)

The best deck I can build from everything except the black-green cards. White supplies the best creatures: a run of cheap flyers, Blossom-Blessed Angel and Saheeli. Red adds the burn (No Admittance and Wrath of the Bloodmane, with Memory Trap and Gideon's Memorial from white) and two Heartstring Pullers. Tap any card to see it full size.
