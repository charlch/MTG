This is the strongest 40 cards in the whole collection, and it is green-white because those are the only two colours with enough good cards and lands. Compared with the first lifegain deck it swaps the weak cards (Living Library, Something Worth Saving, the tricks) for real removal: Helicarrier Strike, Go Nuts!, Sandbenders' Storm and Settle the Wreckage, and adds Jennifer Walters, Knight of Wundagore and Galion for a better curve. The Jace tokens and Lyra still reward every life you gain. Settle the Wreckage is the big defensive card: hold four mana when they swing with everything. Teamwork cards (Helicarrier Strike, Go Nuts!) let you tap creatures with enough total power to get the stronger mode, so keep cheap bodies on the table.
