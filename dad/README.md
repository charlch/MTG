# MTG collection photos (Reality Fracture / FRA)

Photos of my FRA cards, one image per card, plus the original photos they were cut from.

- `cards/` has one image per card (84 cards). Foil and non-foil copies are marked in the file name.
- `tokens-and-extras/` has the tokens, the emblem and the counter sticker sheet.
- `photos/` has the 23 original photos, in the order they were taken (`photo-01.jpg` to `photo-23.jpg`).

The card details are in the Google Sheet "MTG card list - FRA collection".
