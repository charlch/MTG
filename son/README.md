# Son's MTG collection

Photos of Son's cards, one image per card, plus the original photos they were cut from. Most cards are Reality Fracture (FRA); there are also Marvel (MSH, MAR), Avatar (TLA), The Hobbit (HOB), Jumpstart 2025 (J25) and Special Guests (SPG) cards.

**The cards photographed on 28 October 2026 are the whole collection: 128 cards (107 different) plus 6 tokens.** The Copies column counts how many times a card appears in those photos.

- `cards/` has one image per card, `tokens-and-extras/` has the tokens, `photos/` has the original photos, `thumbs/` has the small versions used by the page (made by `tools/build_site.py`), `decks/` has one `.dck` file per deck and `decks.py` has the data for the deck page.
- Foil copies are marked `foil` in the notes.
- See [`../AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md) for how decks are added.
